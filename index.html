Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your iPhone. ReBalance has no servers and your check-ins are never uploaded. That also means your history does not sync between devices, and deleting the app erases it.

How do I delete my data?
Open the Profile tab and tap Delete account. That permanently erases your profile and every check-in stored on the device.

How do I sign out?
Profile → Log out. Your check-in history stays on the device; only the signed-in account is cleared.

Can I stop ReBalance using my Apple or Google account?
Yes. Revoke its access in your Apple ID or Google account settings at any time.

Is ReBalance medical advice?
No. It is a self-reflection and wellness tool, not a medical device, and it is not a substitute for professional care.
