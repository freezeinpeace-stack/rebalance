Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 — MVP draft, review with counsel before launch.

1. What we collect. You sign in with Apple or Google, and ReBalance stores the name and email address that provider shares with us, along with an account identifier. If you use Sign in with Apple you can hide your email, and the app works exactly the same without it. We also store your energy check-in data (answers, energy states, scores, and completed techniques).

2. Where it lives. In this version of the app, all data is stored locally on your device. We operate no servers and your check-ins are never uploaded. The one exception is signing in: that exchange happens directly between your device and Apple or Google, and is governed by their privacy policies.

3. Analytics. This version contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

4. Your controls. You can log out or delete your account at any time from the Profile page. Logging out clears the signed-in account but keeps your history on the device; deleting your account permanently erases everything the app has stored, including your check-in history. You can also revoke the app’s access from your Apple or Google account settings.

5. Not medical care. Check-in data is self-reported wellness information, not a medical record, and the app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

6. Changes. If a future version introduces accounts, sync, or analytics, this policy will be updated and you will be asked to review it before those features activate.

7. Contact. rebalance.app.support@gmail.com
