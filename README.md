# ReBalance — legal pages

Public home for the ReBalance app's Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, so they can
be linked from App Store Connect and from the app itself.

The wording here mirrors the in-app screens (`app/privacy.tsx`, `app/terms.tsx`)
in the ReBalance project. Change both together, or they will drift apart.
